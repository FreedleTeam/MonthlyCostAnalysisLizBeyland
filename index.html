Recap of our conversation

It was great meeting with you today. I also wanted to send over a quick recap of what I heard so you can make sure I took good notes and that I have an accurate understanding of what you’re looking for.

Here’s what I have:

  • Ideally, we’re keeping the purchase price between $500,000 and $600,000, with roughly $150,000 available for the down payment.
  • You need at least two bedrooms plus some kind of additional guest space. You’ll need your own bedroom and a dedicated office, plus somewhere guests can comfortably stay. That third space does not necessarily have to be a traditional bedroom. A finished basement area, nook, or other flexible space could work.
  • Being within about a 20-minute drive of the International School of Denver is important so you can easily pick up Billy. I heard pretty clearly that your relationship with Billy is a major part of how you’re thinking about location.
  • You really like where you live now around 6th and Eudora. You like having a true neighborhood feel while still being able to walk to coffee or easily get somewhere social when you want to be out in the world.
  • You’re open to looking farther east, but you do not want to be in Aurora.
  • A duplex is completely acceptable. You’re in one now and are comfortable sharing one wall.
  • You want a home that has been really well cared for and does not come with a long list of projects or deferred maintenance. Cosmetic updating is totally fine, but you do not want to walk into something that needs rebuilding or major work.
  • Because you’re tired of dealing with maintenance issues, I want us to be particularly thorough during inspection and negotiate strongly around anything meaningful. The goal is for you to feel confident that you’re buying something that should be relatively low maintenance for quite a few years.
  • You’ve owned before in Virginia, so this is not your first home purchase.
  • Entertaining is important to you, especially being able to host your book club. You could have as many as 14 women over at once, and they may get a little loud, so having enough gathering space and not being surrounded by shared walls is important.
  • Along those same lines, it would be nice to have reasonable guest parking nearby.
  • For your own parking, you want at least one reserved space. I’d personally love to find you covered parking if we can make that work.
  • You want some kind of enjoyable outdoor space for yourself, even though you don’t plan on getting another dog.
  • You need a cooling option. Central air would be preferable, but a swamp cooler could also work.
  • You’d also like to stay within a reasonable drive of the Cherry Hills CorePower Yoga.
  • More broadly, I heard that throughout the process you want to know that deadlines are being met, details are being handled, and nothing is falling through the cracks. You want to feel confident that the process is being managed carefully and proactively.

That’s my understanding coming out of our conversation. Take a look and let me know if I missed anything, misunderstood anything, or if there’s something you’d like me to add or adjust.
